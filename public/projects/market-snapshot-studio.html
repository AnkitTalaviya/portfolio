Market Snapshot Studio

Market Snapshot Studio is a reporting concept for turning raw spreadsheet exports into fast executive summaries. It focuses on the last mile of analytics work: taking messy data and producing a clean, shareable snapshot without manual slide-building.

All Projects
CSV in campaign, revenue, or funnel exports
KPI out cards, deltas, anomaly flags, and notes
1 page shareable summary view for busy stakeholders

Experience concept

The product starts with a simple import flow, maps incoming columns to a known metric model, then generates a single report surface with KPI cards, trend narratives, and notable changes. The goal is speed, clarity, and fewer spreadsheet cleanup loops.

Report blocks

  • Period-over-period performance cards.
  • Anomaly detection for spikes and drop-offs.
  • Short written summaries for decision-makers.

Use cases

  • Weekly growth reporting.
  • Campaign performance snapshots.
  • Revenue and conversion pulse checks.

Design goal

Make analytics outputs readable in under five minutes, even for stakeholders who do not want to open raw spreadsheet exports or dashboard builders.

Stack

Vue D3 Serverless Object storage PDF export

Expected outcome

  • Shorter reporting turnaround for non-analyst teams.
  • Cleaner narrative summaries next to the numbers.
  • Consistent one-page reporting across recurring updates.
