Berlin Flat Tracker

Berlin Flat Tracker is a housing-search dashboard built around one real problem: apartment applications scatter across too many tabs, emails, and document folders. This concept turns the whole search process into one visible pipeline.

Core idea

The product combines listing capture, document readiness, and follow-up scheduling in one place. Each apartment entry carries commute hints, rent-fit notes, and status so the search remains organized during high-volume application weeks.

Main features

  • Save listings from multiple portals into one shortlist.
  • Track which documents are ready, missing, or expired.
  • Schedule reminders for replies, viewings, and final follow-ups.

Useful signals

  • Commute range compared with preferred neighborhoods.
  • Monthly budget pressure based on warm rent and deposit.
  • Response-rate trends across portals and listing types.

Why it matters

Housing searches usually fail because the process becomes fragmented. This concept tries to solve the operational side of the search instead of only listing apartments.

Intended outcome
